About

Hi, I'm Jasen.

I like systems where small, simple pieces add up to something strong.

Modular origami

I fold polyhedra, kusudama and tessellations out of many identical paper units. Each unit is easy on its own. The challenge, and the fun, is getting dozens of them to lock together into one stable shape.

Mathlingo

Mathlingo is a math practice app I'm building around repetition. It gives you short daily sessions and uses spaced repetition to bring back what you're about to forget, so progress feels steady instead of stressful.

Right now

Studying at UCLA, folding something new most weeks, and shipping Mathlingo updates one feature at a time.
